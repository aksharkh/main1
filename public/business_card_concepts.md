# AXORAA© Brand Assets: 5 Custom Business Card Concepts

We have designed **5 different custom business card concepts** for AXORAA, mapped directly to modern, high-end, designer-tier layouts. All cards focus on pure printed materiality, tactile paper textures, and integrated themed QR codes without physical die-cuts or stencils.

---

## 1. Concept 1: The "Modular Grid Block"

Inspired by minimalist color-blocked editorial systems, this horizontal design splits the card into contrasting slate-grey and solid neon green blocks.

*   **Stock:** 600gsm natural white cotton textured cardstock.
*   **Front Layout:** A solid 2/3 light warm-grey block on the left and a 1/3 solid vibrant green (`#CCFF00`) block on the right. In the center of the green block sits a large black abstract geometric 'X' logo mark.
*   **Back Layout:** Warm-grey background. Features the vertical text block "LET'S BUILD TOGETHER" repeated down the left margin in dark charcoal. The right side holds contact details aligned to a horizontal grid line, and a clean black QR code.
*   **Edge:** Painted solid neon green color-core.

## 2. Concept 2: The "Tech Console Pass"

Inspired by vertical console badge interfaces, this portrait design features deep black paper, subtle wireframe loops, and structured text grids.

*   **Stock:** Thick 500gsm matte-black recycled fiber paper with rounded corners.
*   **Front Layout:** Vertical layout. The logo "AXORAA©" is printed vertically down the center in bold white. In the background, very subtle dark gray wireframe geometric node line patterns. A solid neon lime green vertical pill bar runs along the right edge.
*   **Back Layout:** Contact details in green monospace typography structured like a system console interface. At the top center, a custom neon green dot-matrix QR code nested in brackets `[ ]`.

## 3. Concept 3: The "Ambient Glow"

Inspired by clean modernist graphics with fluid organic elements, this horizontal design integrates a soft glowing gradient orb in the background.

*   **Stock:** Premium matte-smooth 400gsm black board.
*   **Front Layout:** Minimalist matte black background. A soft, beautifully blurred neon lime green glowing gradient orb occupies the center-right of the card, overlaying a clean, bold white "AXORAA©" logo centered horizontally.
*   **Back Layout:** Matte black background, with contact details printed in clean white sans-serif. The neon green gradient glow bleeds subtly onto the right edge of the card, framing a custom green QR code.

## 4. Concept 4: The "Brutalist Pill Pattern"

Inspired by flat geometric abstraction and asymmetric corner shapes, this landscape card features overlapping capsule paths and high-contrast block screens.

*   **Stock:** Duplexed 500gsm smooth cardstock with a single rounded top-right corner.
*   **Front Layout:** Solid dark charcoal-gray background, featuring an abstract graphic pattern of horizontal rounded capsules (pills) in matte black, overlapping with solid neon lime green and white circles.
*   **Back Layout:** Solid vibrant neon lime green background. The logo "AXORAA©" is printed vertically on the right margin in black ink, with contact info and the QR code printed in clean black.

## 5. Concept 5: The "Pure Typographic"

Inspired by Swiss international-style gridlines and large typography offsets, this horizontal design is partitioned into functional metadata quadrants.

*   **Stock:** Textured 500gsm slate-gray cardstock with solid neon lime green painted core edges.
*   **Front Layout:** A massive, high-contrast neon green wordmark "AXORAA" runs vertically across the card, offset off-center.
*   **Back Layout:** Slate-gray background, divided into four quadrants by thin white sub-millimeter gridlines. The top-left contains a white QR code. The top-right holds monospace sub-headings. The bottom-left holds contact details in white monospace text. The bottom-right is negative space.
